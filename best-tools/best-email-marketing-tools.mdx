## How this ranking works

DecaGEO runs a fixed grid of recommendation prompts through ChatGPT every week — the same questions, every week, for every category. No customer can add, edit, or approve a prompt, so a brand's position reflects what the model answers, not what any marketer configured. [DECA Score](https://decageo.ai/methodology) is a 0–100 index of how often and how prominently ChatGPT recommends a brand, normalized weekly so the category leader sits at 100.

## The longest #1 on any board, and a new #2

Brevo has held #1 in every tracked week since May 10, 2026 — through the GPT-5.4 spring and the switch to GPT-5.6 in late July. No other category leader has that record.

The #2 spot did change. MailerLite held it through the spring, then Klaviyo climbed from #6 in mid-July to #2 in four weeks, reaching 99.0 against Brevo's 100 in the week of August 2. The gap re-opened to 7.5 points the following week, so the challenge stands as a confirmed climb that stopped short of the top. The mid-list below them (Customer.io, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Omnisend) swings week to week, largely in step with the same brands' moves in [Marketing Automation](https://decageo.ai/docs/best-tools/best-marketing-automation-tools).

The swings are the [AI Ranking Movers series](/ai-ranking-changes/2026-08-09)' story. This page's story is that the cast barely changes: nine of the spring's top ten are still in the top ten (Iterable slipped just outside, Omnisend entered). The order reshuffles weekly — the shortlist doesn't.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of Email Marketing recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT (US region) and records which brands are named and how prominently.

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, normalized weekly so the leader sits at 100.
- **Current ranking:** week of August 9, 2026, collected on GPT-5.6. Historical references from May 10 – July 19, 2026 were collected on GPT-5.4; comparisons across late July cross that model boundary.
- **Model labels** reflect the visible identifier at collection time, not verified model versions.
- **Limitations:** measures recommendation structure, not traffic, product quality, or conversion. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend.
