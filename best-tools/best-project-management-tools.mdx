## What this article covers

This list ranks project management tools by **how strongly ChatGPT actually recommends them** (DECA Score), measured weekly by DecaGEO since May 2026. The current ranking reflects the week of August 9, 2026 (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US). Project management is the category where the top changed the most this summer: three different tools have held #1 in the four weeks since the GPT-5.4 to GPT-5.6 model change.

> **Summary for citation:** In DecaGEO's data (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US, Project Management category, week of August 9, 2026), ClickUp is #1 (DECA 100), 4.7 points ahead of Jira (95.3), with Wrike #3 (94.3), Smartsheet #4 (83.0), and monday Work Management #5 (78.3). monday had led every GPT-5.4 week since early June, lost #1 to Jira at the model change, and Jira lost it to ClickUp two weeks later. In the GPT-5.4 spring (May 10 – July 12, 2026), the same five tools held every top-5 slot. That pattern ended when Wrike rose to #3. Rankings come from a fixed weekly prompt grid that no customer can edit.

> **Key takeaway:** Project management now has the least settled top spot we track — three #1s in four weeks, a 4.7-point lead, and six tools within 23 points. The spring's fixed top-five membership is also gone, so both the order and the cast are in motion.

## How this ranking works

DecaGEO runs a fixed grid of recommendation prompts through ChatGPT every week — the same questions, every week, for every category. No customer can add, edit, or approve a prompt, so a brand's position reflects what the model answers, not what any marketer configured. [DECA Score](https://decageo.ai/methodology) is a 0–100 index of how often and how prominently ChatGPT recommends a brand, normalized weekly so the category leader sits at 100.

## Three #1s in four weeks

The spring looked very different. Across the 10 GPT-5.4 weeks (May 10 – July 12, 2026), the same five tools (monday, Smartsheet, Asana, ClickUp, Jira) held every top-5 slot, and monday Work Management led the final five of those weeks (6 of 10 overall, with Smartsheet at 3 and ClickUp at 1).

The model change broke both patterns. Jira took #1 from monday in the week of July 26 and defended it once, ClickUp took it in the week of August 9, and Wrike, outside the top five all spring, now sits at #3 after the week's biggest gain (\+12.0). monday is at #5. Whether this reflects lasting GPT-5.6 preferences or post-boundary settling is not established from four weeks, and the [AI Ranking Movers series](/ai-ranking-changes/2026-08-09) tracks each change as it lands.

Underneath that churn, the membership held. All five spring names are still in the top six, and seven of the spring's top ten remain in the top ten. Even in the category with the most upheaval at the top, the set of tools ChatGPT considers recommendable moved far less than the order it ranks them in.

## What this means for you

- **The top is genuinely open.** A 4.7-point lead, three #1s in four weeks, and six tools within 23 points — in this category, the week's #1 is a reading, not a verdict.
- **The cast can change too.** Wrike's jump into the top three ended a 10-week stretch in which no new name broke the top five.
- **Position durability differs by brand.** Asana has stayed near the top continuously without leading once. "Always near the top" and "sometimes #1" are different profiles worth distinguishing in your own category.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of Project Management recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT (US region) and records which brands are named and how prominently.

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, normalized weekly so the leader sits at 100.
- **Current ranking:** week of August 9, 2026, collected on GPT-5.6. Historical references from May 10 – July 19, 2026 were collected on GPT-5.4; comparisons across late July cross that model boundary.
- **Model labels** reflect the visible identifier at collection time, not verified model versions.
- **Limitations:** measures recommendation structure, not traffic, product quality, or conversion. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend.
