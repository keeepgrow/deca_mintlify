## What this article covers

This list ranks project management tools by **how strongly ChatGPT actually recommends them** (DECA Score), measured weekly by DecaGEO since May 2026. The current ranking reflects the week of August 16, 2026 (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US). Project management is the category where the top changes the most: three #1 changes in the four weeks since the GPT-5.4 to GPT-5.6 model change, with Jira and ClickUp trading the spot.

> **Summary for citation:** In DecaGEO's data (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US, Project Management category, week of August 16, 2026), Jira is #1 (DECA 100), 7.6 points ahead of ClickUp (92.4), after retaking the spot ClickUp held for one week. monday Work Management and Asana are tied at #3 (77.1) with Smartsheet at #5 (74.3) — the same five tools that held every top-5 slot in the GPT-5.4 spring (May 10 – July 12, 2026). No leader has held this board longer than two weeks since the model change. Rankings come from a fixed weekly prompt grid that no customer can edit.

> **Key takeaway:** The order churns, the shortlist doesn't. The spring's five names are the current top five again after a month of #1 flips and Wrike's one-week visit to #3 — in this category, which tools ChatGPT considers top-tier is far more stable than the order it puts them in.

## How this ranking works

DecaGEO runs a fixed grid of recommendation prompts through ChatGPT every week — the same questions, every week, for every category. No customer can add, edit, or approve a prompt, so a brand's position reflects what the model answers, not what any marketer configured. [DECA Score](https://decageo.ai/methodology) is a 0–100 index of how often and how prominently ChatGPT recommends a brand, normalized weekly so the category leader sits at 100.

## Three #1 changes in four weeks

The spring looked very different. Across the 10 GPT-5.4 weeks (May 10 – July 12, 2026), the same five tools (monday, Smartsheet, Asana, ClickUp, Jira) held every top-5 slot, and monday Work Management led the final five of those weeks (6 of 10 overall, with Smartsheet at 3 and ClickUp at 1).

The model change ended monday's run and started a rotation that has not stopped. Jira took #1 in the week of July 26 and held two weeks, ClickUp took it in the week of August 9, and Jira took it back a week later. No leader has held longer than two weeks since the change. The same week Jira retook the top, Wrike gave back its one-week climb to #3 in full (\+12.0 in, −23.9 out), the sharpest single-brand reversal we have tracked in this category.

Underneath that churn, the membership held — and this week, precisely. The current top five are the spring's five names, back in a new order, and seven of the spring's top ten remain in the top ten. The weekly moves are the [AI Ranking Movers series](/ai-ranking-changes/2026-08-16)' story. This page's story is that the set of tools ChatGPT considers recommendable barely moves even while its order refuses to settle.

## What this means for you

- **The top is genuinely open.** Three #1 changes in four weeks, a 7.6-point lead, and no reign longer than two weeks — in this category, the week's #1 is a reading, not a verdict.
- **One-week spikes reverse.** Wrike's \+12.0-then-−23.9 is the cleanest example yet of why a single strong week reads as an observation, not a trend.
- **Membership is the durable signal.** The spring five are the current top five. If your brand competes here, tier membership is the metric to defend; order will keep moving on its own.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of Project Management recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT (US region) and records which brands are named and how prominently.

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, normalized weekly so the leader sits at 100.
- **Current ranking:** week of August 16, 2026, collected on GPT-5.6. Historical references from May 10 – July 19, 2026 were collected on GPT-5.4; comparisons across late July cross that model boundary.
- **Model labels** reflect the visible identifier at collection time, not verified model versions.
- **Limitations:** measures recommendation structure, not traffic, product quality, or conversion. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend.
