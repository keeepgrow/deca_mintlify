This category spent the spring as the most settled board we track, then produced its first #1 change at the summer's model boundary.

## How this ranking works

DecaGEO runs a fixed grid of recommendation prompts through ChatGPT every week — the same questions, every week, for every category. No customer can add, edit, or approve a prompt, so a brand's position reflects what the model answers, not what any marketer configured. [DECA Score](https://decageo.ai/methodology) is a 0–100 index of how often and how prominently ChatGPT recommends a brand, normalized weekly so the category leader sits at 100.

## A long reign, a two-week interruption, and a return

CreatorIQ held #1 in every GPT-5.4 week from May 10 through July 19, 2026 — one of the most settled leads on any board. The first change came with the model: in the week of July 26, the first on GPT-5.6, Aspire took #1 and then defended it for a second week by 1.8 points.

The week of August 9 reversed it. CreatorIQ retook #1 by 9.4 points, making Aspire's reign a two-week interruption rather than a new order. One split makes the top two worth watching in both columns: Aspire remains the most frequently named platform (56% of responses), while CreatorIQ wins on prominence — the same divergence between frequency and position that shows up in other categories' top pairs.

The #1 drama is the [AI Ranking Movers series](/ai-ranking-changes/2026-08-09)' story. This page's story is the membership: nine of the spring's top ten platforms are still in the top ten (Upfluence slipped out, HypeAuditor entered). The set of platforms ChatGPT considers recommendable held through a model change — only the order at the very top moved.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of Influencer Marketing recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT (US region) and records which brands are named and how prominently.

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, normalized weekly so the leader sits at 100.
- **Current ranking:** week of August 9, 2026, collected on GPT-5.6. Historical references from May 10 – July 19, 2026 were collected on GPT-5.4; comparisons across late July cross that model boundary.
- **Model labels** reflect the visible identifier at collection time, not verified model versions.
- **Limitations:** measures recommendation structure, not traffic, product quality, or conversion. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend.
