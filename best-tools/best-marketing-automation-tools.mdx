## What this article covers

This list ranks marketing automation tools by **how strongly ChatGPT actually recommends them** (DECA Score), measured weekly by DecaGEO since May 2026. The current ranking reflects the week of August 9, 2026 (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US). Marketing automation spent the spring as the most bunched top of any category. The summer split it into a clear leader and a crowded chase pack.

> **Summary for citation:** In DecaGEO's data (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US, Marketing Automation category, week of August 9, 2026), Braze is #1 (DECA 100) with a 20-point lead over Customer.io (80.0), and the #2–#6 pack sits within 7.4 points: Customer.io, then HubSpot Marketing Hub and Klaviyo tied at 77.9, then Agentforce Marketing and Iterable tied at 72.6. In the GPT-5.4 spring (May 10 – July 12, 2026), three tools held #1 (Braze 7 weeks, HubSpot 2, Adobe Marketo Engage 1). Braze lost the spot once more in mid-July and has led every week since the model change. Rankings come from a fixed weekly prompt grid that no customer can edit.

> **Key takeaway:** The category now has two separate races. Braze has led every GPT-5.6 week by double digits, and the real weekly contest is the #2–#6 pack, where four brands sit within 7.4 points and the week's biggest gains and falls regularly come from the same names.

## How this ranking works

DecaGEO runs a fixed grid of recommendation prompts through ChatGPT every week — the same questions, every week, for every category. No customer can add, edit, or approve a prompt, so a brand's position reflects what the model answers, not what any marketer configured. [DECA Score](https://decageo.ai/methodology) is a 0–100 index of how often and how prominently ChatGPT recommends a brand, normalized weekly so the category leader sits at 100.

## A leader that pulled away, and a pack that keeps churning

In the GPT-5.4 spring (May 10 – July 12, 2026), this was the most bunched top of any category — at one point six tools within 12 points, with three different #1s across the window (Braze 7 weeks, HubSpot Marketing Hub 2, Adobe Marketo Engage 1). HubSpot took the top once more in mid-July.

Since the model change, Braze has led every week and the lead has held around 20 points. The bunching moved down a tier: Customer.io, HubSpot, Klaviyo, Agentforce Marketing, and Iterable now form a #2–#6 pack within 7.4 points, and it is the most reversal-prone group we track. In the week of August 9, four of the ten biggest gains across all categories came from this board — Agentforce Marketing (\+24.4) and Klaviyo (\+23.3) among them, both reversing falls of similar size from the week before. Klaviyo alone has posted five sharp direction changes in six weeks here, while climbing steadily to #2 in Email Marketing over the same stretch.

Those reversals are the [AI Ranking Movers series](/ai-ranking-changes/2026-08-09)' story. This page's story is the opposite one: every one of the spring's top ten is still in the top ten, the only board in this series with full membership retention (Adobe Journey Optimizer joined through a tie at #10). The most volatile order we track sits on the most stable shortlist.

## What this means for you

- **Two races, two readings.** Braze's lead has been stable for three weeks, while the #2–#6 pack repriced twice in that time. Which race a brand is in changes how much a weekly move means.
- **This board's swings are the sharpest we track.** Gains of 20-plus points here have reversed within a week more than once, so a single strong week reads as an observation, not a trend.
- **Cross-board profiles matter.** Klaviyo, Braze, Customer.io, HubSpot, and Brevo all appear on the Email board too, usually with a different rank — worth checking both if the brand competes in both.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of Marketing Automation recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT (US region) and records which brands are named and how prominently.

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, normalized weekly so the leader sits at 100.
- **Current ranking:** week of August 9, 2026, collected on GPT-5.6. Historical references from May 10 – July 19, 2026 were collected on GPT-5.4; comparisons across late July cross that model boundary.
- **Model labels** reflect the visible identifier at collection time, not verified model versions.
- **Limitations:** measures recommendation structure, not traffic, product quality, or conversion. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend.
