## What this article covers

This list ranks CRM software by **how strongly ChatGPT actually recommends it** (DECA Score), measured weekly by DecaGEO since May 2026. The current ranking reflects the week of August 9, 2026 (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US). CRM has changed character in that time: the spring's most contested #1 has become one of the most settled boards we track.

> **Summary for citation:** In DecaGEO's data (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US, CRM category, week of August 9, 2026), Dynamics 365 Sales is #1 (DECA 100) for a fifth straight week. Agentforce Sales is #2 (87.4) after passing Zoho CRM (#3, 85.0), and HubSpot Sales Hub is #4 (82.7). In the GPT-5.4 spring (May 10 – July 12, 2026), the #1 spot flipped 7 times in 10 weeks between Dynamics (6 weeks) and Zoho (4). Rankings come from a fixed weekly prompt grid that no customer can edit.

> **Key takeaway:** The CRM board settled. Dynamics 365 Sales has held #1 since mid-July, no CRM brand moved ±10 ranks in either of the last two weeks, and the live contest is now #2 — where Agentforce Sales just displaced Zoho CRM.

## How this ranking works

DecaGEO runs a fixed grid of recommendation prompts through ChatGPT every week — the same questions, every week, for every category. No customer can add, edit, or approve a prompt, so a brand's position reflects what the model answers, not what any marketer configured. [DECA Score](https://decageo.ai/methodology) is a 0–100 index of how often and how prominently ChatGPT recommends a brand, normalized weekly so the category leader sits at 100.

## From the most contested #1 to a settled one

In the GPT-5.4 spring (May 10 – July 12, 2026), CRM had the least stable top spot of any category we track. The #1 changed hands 7 times in 10 weeks, with Dynamics 365 Sales holding 6 weeks and Zoho CRM 4, on nearly identical average scores.

That race ended in mid-July. Dynamics has now held #1 for five straight weeks (July 12 – August 9), across the GPT-5.4 to GPT-5.6 model change, and no CRM brand moved ±10 ranks in either of the last two weeks — the most stable board in our tracking. The movement shifted to #2: Agentforce Sales passed Zoho CRM in the week of August 9, and a HubSpot Sales Hub challenge (#2 in the week of July 26) dissolved within a week.

One nuance keeps the board interesting. Zoho CRM is still the most frequently named CRM (57% of responses against Dynamics' 53%), but it sits at #3 because it tends to be named lower in the answer. Frequency and prominence are different contests.

Those weekly moves are the [AI Ranking Movers series](/ai-ranking-changes/2026-08-09)' story. This page's story is the membership: nine of the spring's top ten CRMs are still in the top ten (monday CRM dropped out, SAP Sales Cloud entered), across a model change and a #1 that flipped seven times. Who ChatGPT considers a top CRM barely changes — what changes is the order it puts them in.

## What this means for you

- **The top is settled, the #2 race is live.** Dynamics' five-week hold makes it the current default AI answer for CRM, and the #2–#4 pack sits within 5 points of each other.
- **Frequency and prominence diverge here.** Zoho is named most often yet ranks #3 — a brand can win one contest and not the other.
- **A stable board can still reprice a brand quickly.** HubSpot's one-week stay at #2 shows single-week moves read better as observations than as trends.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of CRM recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT (US region) and records which brands are named and how prominently.

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, normalized weekly so the leader sits at 100.
- **Current ranking:** week of August 9, 2026, collected on GPT-5.6. Historical references from May 10 – July 19, 2026 were collected on GPT-5.4; comparisons across late July cross that model boundary.
- **Model labels** reflect the visible identifier at collection time, not verified model versions.
- **Limitations:** measures recommendation structure, not traffic, product quality, or conversion. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend.
