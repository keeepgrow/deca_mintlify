Segment Position is your rank inside a specific buyer context, rather than the overall category chart. Because DecaGEO's queries are generated from buyer personas with conditions defined per category — the CRM chart, for example, filters by business scale, use case, and integration — every brand also has a rank for each of those segments.

## Overview

DecaGEO's segment filters don't just narrow results — they simulate different buyers. Filtering by a segment replays only the queries that match that buyer context. You're not slicing one dataset differently; you're seeing what AI recommends when the question comes from a different type of buyer.

<Info>
  Segment filters are available on every category chart. The segments themselves follow the decision conditions that matter in each category, so they vary from one chart to another.
</Info>

## How Segment Position is determined

```text
Segment Position = rank by DECA Score, computed only from responses
                   to the queries matching that segment's buyer context
```

The scoring is identical to the overall chart — same points, same exclusion rules — applied to a narrower pool of responses.

> **Example:** "What CRM tools work best for a small marketing team on a tight budget?" and "What enterprise CRM platforms offer advanced automation for large sales teams?" are different personas asking the same category. Each answer counts toward its own segment — and AI's recommendations often differ between the two.

## Why Segment Position matters

### Buyers don't ask generic questions

Real buyers ask with their situation attached. Segment Position mirrors those situational questions, so it's closer to what your actual buyers see than any overall number.

### It shows where you actually win

A brand that ranks #1 overall might drop to #5 for enterprise buyers — and vice versa. The segment view reveals the markets where AI already prefers you, and the ones where you're invisible.

### It tells you where to focus

A near-win segment is a sharper target than a whole category. It also surfaces segments no product has locked down yet — open ground you can claim first.
