Rankings refresh every week, which means your work gets a scoreboard every week. This is the loop DecaGEO is built around — four steps, each living in a screen you've already seen. Run it weekly; the order is the point.

## Step 1: Diagnose where your visibility comes from

Two tabs on your (or your closest competitor's) product details answer it:

- **[Off-Page Citations](/using/off-page-citations)** — which reviews, forums, and media AI cites in your category, and the *Not Mentioned* list: sources AI already trusts where you have no presence. That list is your PR and reviews target queue.
- **[On-Page GEO Patterns](/using/on-page-geo-patterns)** — what the most-cited pages in your category do on-page that yours don't, down to the HTML.

## Step 2: Verify what AI actually says

Before changing anything, read the source material: **[Prompt & AI Response](/using/prompt-and-ai-response)** shows the buyer questions behind your category's ranking and how AI answered — who it shortlists, in what order, and in its own words. This is the reality your Step 1 findings have to explain.

## Step 3: Act on this week's plays

Your **[Dashboard](/using/dashboard)** turns the diagnosis into an ordered to-do list:

- Start with **Category Basics** — the fundamentals almost every top-ranked product in your category already has, sorted by how widespread each is.
- Then take the **Weekly Playbook** — the moves behind this week's actual breakouts, ranked by impact and effort.

Every action ships with a copy-paste AI prompt that applies the play to your own page — the exact rewrite, tag, or markup it calls for.

## Step 4: Track whether it worked

Give a change a few weekly cycles, then check two places:

- **[Chart History](/using/chart-history)** — did your rank, DECA Score, and Mention Rate actually move, and is it holding?
- **[Compare](/using/compare)** — line yourself up against the products above you and watch the signal tags shift from *Opportunity* and *Competitive* toward *Shared*.

Then the loop restarts: this week's chart is next week's diagnosis.
