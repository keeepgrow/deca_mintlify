This takes about one minute, with no sign-up — the rankings are already measured and refreshed weekly, so checking your brand is a search, not a setup.

## Step 1: Search your brand

**Your task:** type your brand name or domain into the search bar on [decageo.ai](https://decageo.ai) and hit **Check Ranking**.

**Search** in the header opens the same search from any page. Searching a company name (e.g. *HubSpot*) works as well as a product name (e.g. *HubSpot Sales Hub*).

## Step 2: Read your result

The results page splits in two, and so does your path:

**If your brand is ranked**, you'll see its products — each with the categories it charts in — and its company under **Vendors**. From any product card, **View Full Category Rankings** opens the chart with your row highlighted and a summary card up top (your rank, [DECA Score](/metrics/deca-score), and [Mention Rate](/metrics/mention-rate)), and **View Product Details** jumps straight to the evidence.

**If it isn't on any chart — yet**, that means AI isn't recommending the brand often enough to rank it. That's not a dead end; it's a starting line. [Break Into the AI Ranking](/get-started/break-into-the-ranking) walks you through finding out why and what to change, the same page shows the brands that weren't ranked last week and exactly what moved them onto the chart, and [adding your brand](/using/add-your-brand) gets you notified the moment AI starts recommending it.

## Step 3: Open your product details

**Your task:** click **View Product Details** on your (or a competitor's) product.

This is where the numbers turn into evidence — history, segments, citations, on-page patterns, and the AI's actual answers. The next guide walks you through reading all of it.
