Exploring DecaGEO is free: every weekly ranking, in every category, with no sign-up. **Pro** unlocks the full depth behind the rankings, and **Enterprise** is built around your categories and your team.

## Free

- Weekly AI visibility rankings across every category — every brand, every rank
- Recent chart history with filters
- Top winning segments and weekly breakout highlights
- Top cited sources behind each product's AI mentions
- Track a product, and [Add Your Brand](/using/add-your-brand) alerts
- [Compare](/using/compare) products side by side across rankings and segments

## Pro

Everything in Free, plus:

- Full [chart history](/using/chart-history) (Rank, DECA Score, Mention Rate)
- Citation coverage: every domain and every URL behind each mention
- [On-Page GEO Patterns](/using/on-page-geo-patterns) — what AI cites, and what's missing from your pages
- Action Plan — Category Basics and Weekly Playbook on your [Dashboard](/using/dashboard), with copy-paste AI prompts
- Unlimited tracking with weekly change alerts
- PDF & CSV exports for executive-ready reports
- Compare citations and GEO patterns across products, side by side

## Enterprise

Need coverage beyond the current categories, or a plan for a larger team? Enterprise plans are built to fit — [tell us what you're looking for](https://decageo.ai/pricing).
