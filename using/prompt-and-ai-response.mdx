The Prompt & AI Response tab is the raw material behind a product's ranking: the buyer-style prompts DecaGEO asked that week, and how AI answered each one — which brands it recommended, in what order, in its own words. Every number on the chart traces back to a response you can read here.

  ![Prompt & AI Response tab with prompts and brand ranking trails](/images/docs/prompt-ai-response.png)
</Frame>

## The prompt list

The tab lists every prompt whose response mentioned the product, with the week's totals — prompts, responses, and mentions — up top. Each entry shows:

- **Persona tags** — the buyer conditions the prompt was generated from (for a CRM prompt: use case, integration, and business scale). No two prompts ask as the same buyer; see [how the queries are designed](https://decageo.ai/methodology).
- **The prompt itself** — the full buyer question, verbatim.
- **The result row** — the AI platform and model version, the **Brand Ranking Trail** (every recommended brand in answer order, with this product highlighted at its [position](/metrics/deca-score)), and the execution date.

Use **Filters** to narrow by AI platform, country, or market segment, and **Export CSV** [Pro] to take the table with you.

## The AI Response panel

Click any result row to open the response panel: the prompt, the **Mentioned Brands** in their recommendation order, and the AI's answer. The full response text — what AI actually said about each brand, in its own words — is available on [Pro](/deca/support-hub/plans-and-pricing).
