The Chart History tab shows a product's weekly movement across Rank, [DECA Score](/metrics/deca-score), and [Mention Rate](/metrics/mention-rate) in its category — the view that separates a one-week spike from real momentum.

<Frame caption="Chart History for a ranked product">
  ![Chart History tab showing DECA Score and Mention Rate movement](/images/docs/chart-history.png)
</Frame>

## The movement chart

Pick a range — **Last 4 Weeks**, **Last 8 Weeks**, or **All** — and the chart plots DECA Score and Mention Rate week by week across it.

## The weekly table

Below the chart, every measured week is a row with the exact numbers behind the lines:

- **Week** — the collection week.
- **AI Platform** — the engine and the exact model version that measured that week (e.g. ChatGPT, gpt-5.6-terra).
- **Rank**, **DECA Score**, and **Mention Rate** — each with its change from the week before.

Because every row records the model that produced it, you can always tell whether a movement happened within the same measurement conditions or across a model transition.
