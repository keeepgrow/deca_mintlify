The Dashboard turns each week's chart movement into a plan you can act on. Open it from **Dashboard** in the header, pick a category and week, and work through its three tabs — who broke out, what they did, and the exact moves to copy.

<Tip>
  Not sure where to look first? Start with the [GEO Guide](/using/geo-guide) section at the top of this tab. Answer a short run of questions and you end up with a list of what's holding your own site back — and each item points you at the tab below that shows how to fix it.
</Tip>

## Weekly Breakouts

The opening tab answers *who's worth copying this week*:

- **What to watch this week** — the week's counts: products ranked, new entries, and top movers.
- **Who to copy this week** — each breakout with a one-line summary of what changed (e.g. *10 new on-page GEO patterns*, *2 new reference citations*), alongside its rank and mention rate movement.
- **Each breakout up close** — open a card to see the specific evidence behind that breakout: newly cited pages and the new on-page patterns or citations they picked up, with a jump to that product's [On-Page GEO Patterns](/using/on-page-geo-patterns). When this week's breakouts share a pattern, the shared play is called out.

The GEO Guide sends you here from *Where else does AI hear your name?* — the breakouts are where you see which sources moved a product this week.

## Category Basics [Pro]

The fundamentals almost every top-ranked brand in the category shares — the floor you need before anything else. Each basic is ranked by coverage across the category's top 10 leaders (*"94% of the top 10 brands have this covered"*) and tagged by **effort** and **impact**, so you close the most widespread gaps first.

Expand any item for **Why it matters**, **How to apply it**, and a ready-to-run **AI Prompt** — copy it, paste in your page URL or content, and AI applies the play for you: the exact rewrite, tag, or markup it calls for. The list updates weekly against the category's leaders.

**Arriving from the GEO Guide.** When a prescription in the [GEO Guide](/using/geo-guide) sends you here, the URL carries an `#on:<item>` fragment: that basic opens expanded and highlighted, and a banner at the top names the item that sent you and links back to where you left the Guide.

## Weekly Playbook [Pro]

The moves behind this week's breakouts, ranked by impact and effort so you know where to start. Each play shows which movers applied it, why it matters, and how to apply it — with the same copy-paste AI prompt — plus **Proof this week**: the movers' actual cited pages, linking into their on-page patterns.

<Frame caption="A play in the Weekly Playbook, expanded">
  ![An expanded Weekly Playbook play with why it matters, how to apply it, and proof](/images/docs/dashboard-weekly-playbook.png)
</Frame>

The playbook rebuilds every week as the movers change, so it always reflects what's currently working in your category.

**Arriving from the GEO Guide.** Plays link the same way as Category Basics — the play opens expanded and highlighted, under a banner naming what sent you. Because the playbook rebuilds each week, the play you were sent to may not be in the current week's list; when that happens the banner says so and points you at a category that still has it.
