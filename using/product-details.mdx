Every product on an [AI Ranking Chart](/using/ai-ranking-chart) has a detail page — open it by clicking the product's row, or from a brand search. The chart tells you where a product stands; the detail page holds the evidence behind that position.

At the top you'll find the product's company, its site URL, and its categories, along with the **week selector**, a **Track** button, and **Export PDF** [Pro] — a report covering the full page, or one focused on on-page GEO patterns.

## The Overview tab

The Overview tab opens with **Weekly Metric Performance** — the product's Rank, [DECA Score](/metrics/deca-score), and [Mention Rate](/metrics/mention-rate) for the selected week, each with its change — followed by previews of the product's recent chart movement, top segments, and on-page GEO focus. Each preview links to its full tab with **View Details**, and the page closes with the category's current Top 3.

## The analysis tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Chart History](/using/chart-history) | DECA Score and Mention Rate week over week — spike or momentum? |
| [Segment Position](/using/segment-position-tab) | The product's rank and mention rate in each buyer segment |
| [Off-Page Citations](/using/off-page-citations) | The reviews, forums, and media AI cites when it recommends the product |
| [On-Page GEO Patterns](/using/on-page-geo-patterns) **[Pro]** | The content, metadata, and technical patterns of its most-cited pages |
| [Prompt & AI Response](/using/prompt-and-ai-response) | The buyer-style prompts and AI answers behind the ranking |

## Brand Hub

Every company has a **Brand Hub** — the company-level view behind any single product. Open it from the company link at the top of a product page (e.g. **By HubSpot**) to see all of the company's products and every category they show up in.

## Track the products you care about

Click **Track** on any product to bookmark it. Tracked products appear together on the **Tracked Products** page — each with its rank, rank change, and mention change for the week — which you can open anytime from your profile menu (**Recent Tracks → View All**).

You can track a product that isn't ranked yet: its row shows a dash until the product lands on a chart, then fills in with its position.
