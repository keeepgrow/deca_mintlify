The Segment Position tab shows every segment where a product appears in AI responses — and where it ranks in each. This is the per-product view of the metric; for what a segment is and how segment ranks are computed, see [Segment Position](/metrics/segment-position) in Metrics.

It is also where to look when you don't know what words to describe your own buyer with. The segments here are the buyer types AI has already split the category into — company size, use case, integration need — written in AI's words rather than yours. If you're filling in *who is it for, and what do they need* in the [GEO Guide](/using/geo-guide), or writing the copy that has to match, borrow the wording from a segment your product belongs in.

## The segment grid

Segments are grouped by the category's condition axes — the CRM chart, for example, groups them by business scale, use case, and integration. Each card is a segment the product is mentioned in, showing:

- the product's **rank** within that segment
- its **mention rate** among that segment's responses

## Reading the colors

Cards are color-coded by mention rate, from the deepest shade down to *Not Mentioned* — the legend below the grid shows the bands. One scan tells you where the product is strongest, where it barely registers, and which segments it hasn't entered at all.
