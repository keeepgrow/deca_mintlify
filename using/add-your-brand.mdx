Every chart includes every brand AI recommends — there is no artificial cutoff. If your brand isn't on its category chart, AI hasn't recommended it as a solution in that category's context yet. **Add Your Brand** registers it, so you hear the moment that changes.

## Register your brand

Click **+ Add your Brand** in the header — it's also offered right on the search results page when the brand you searched isn't ranked. Three details are all it takes: the brand name, its website URL, and a business email. You can submit with or without an account.

After each weekly refresh, if your brand has landed on a chart, you get an email — and an in-app notification if you're signed in. If the brand was already registered, the form catches the duplicate and points you to **Settings → Brand** to check or edit it instead.

## What registering does — and doesn't — change

Registering doesn't put your brand into the ranking. The prompts are brand-anonymous and the measurement stays organic, so a brand enters a chart only when AI actually starts recommending it. What registration changes is that you'll know the moment it happens — no need to keep checking the chart.

In the meantime, the [Dashboard](/using/dashboard) shows what this week's breakouts in your category did to get there, and the [GEO Playbooks](/geo-playbook/geo-cheat-keys/fundamentals/what-is-geo) cover the groundwork you can start on your own pages.

## Market not covered? Request a Chart

If what's missing isn't your brand but the chart itself, use **Request a Chart** in the header. The request covers more than a category: pick the industry and the specific category you want ranked, and optionally a target country and additional AI platforms to analyze — every chart includes ChatGPT and the US market by default.

Coverage expands based on demand, and new charts are announced in the [changelog](/deca/whats-new/changelog).
