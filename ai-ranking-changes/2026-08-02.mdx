## What this article covers

This is the second week on GPT-5.6 and the first where both sides of the comparison run on the same model, so normal week-over-week reading resumes here. One change stood out anyway: [Profound](https://decageo.ai/products/profound/category/geo-generative-engine-optimization) took [GEO](https://decageo.ai/category/geo-generative-engine-optimization) #1 from Semrush, a spot Semrush had held in every previous week of our tracking. Beyond that, the week was the quietest in our tracking (9.7% of brands moved ±10 ranks), and the questions the model change opened last week got their first answers: the smaller recommendation set held, all three new #1s defended their spots, and two of last week's near-flips reverted. Below: the three biggest moves, verdicts on last week's flags, a category-by-category opportunity map, and how to read a one-week change.

> **Summary for citation:** In DecaGEO's week of August 2, 2026 data (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US, 10 categories, 421 ranked brand slots), Profound took GEO #1 from Semrush (now #2, 94.1), the first GEO-native tool to lead the GEO board in our tracking. The recommendation set rose from 399 to 421 but stayed below its GPT-5.4 range (439–493) for a second week, consistent with a smaller GPT-5.6 baseline. All three #1s that changed at the model boundary held their spots (Braze, Jira, Aspire), while two challenges from that week reverted: Help Desk's gap re-opened from 2 to 7 points and CRM's from 7 to 18, with HubSpot Sales Hub falling to #4 (−21.5, the week's biggest fall). Klaviyo closed within 1.1 points of Brevo Marketing Platform for Email #1 after a four-week climb. The DECA Volatility Index hit 9.7%, the lowest in our tracking.

> **Key takeaway:** Two GPT-5.6 weeks now separate signal from model-change noise. The smaller recommendation set and the three new #1s persisted, so they read as the GPT-5.6 baseline. The Help Desk and CRM challenges did not, so they read as one-week moves. A single week's move is an observation, not a signal to act on — this week's verdicts are exactly why.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT in the US region and records which brands are named and how prominently, across 10 software categories. This week's data was collected on July 30, 2026 on GPT-5.6 (model label `gpt-5.6-terra`), the second GPT-5.6 collection. Both weeks compared here ran on the same model with the same prompt grid (714 responses each), so week-over-week figures no longer cross a model boundary. References to GPT-5.4 levels use the six-week range from June 14 to July 19, 2026.

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, based on how frequently and how prominently a brand is named. Scores are normalized weekly so each category's leader sits at 100. A score change therefore reads as a change in the gap to the leader, not an absolute shift.
- **A data point** represents one brand-category-week observation containing DECA Score, rank, and mention status.
- **Model labels** (e.g., GPT-5.6) reflect the visible model identifier at the time of each weekly collection, not independently verified model versions.
- **Flag verdicts** compare the second GPT-5.6 week against both the first and the GPT-5.4 range. One confirming week is a first read, and verdicts stay open to revision as weeks accumulate. Rank-move counts (±10) are computed among brands present in both weeks (320 this week).
- **Limitations:** Week-over-week counts partly reflect newly detected brands and may include re-aggregation noise. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend. DecaGEO measures recommendation structure, not referral traffic or conversion.
