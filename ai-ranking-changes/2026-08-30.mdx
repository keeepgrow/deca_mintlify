## What this article covers

Each week this report separates what held from what changed. This week read like a test of last week's changes, and most of them failed it: [Dynamics 365 Sales](https://decageo.ai/products/dynamics-365-sales/category/crm) retook [CRM](https://decageo.ai/category/crm) #1 one week after losing it, and the [Help Desk](https://decageo.ai/category/help-desk) tie resolved back to [Zendesk Suite](https://decageo.ai/products/zendesk-suite/category/help-desk). What survived the test: [ClickUp](https://decageo.ai/products/clickup/category/project-management)'s [Project Management](https://decageo.ai/category/project-management) defense, only the second back-to-back #1 hold of the GPT-5.6 era. Meanwhile the quietest race got loud in a different way — [Klaviyo](https://decageo.ai/products/klaviyo/category/email-marketing) closed the Email mention-rate gap to 1.3 points. Below: the biggest moves, the held-vs-changed table, a category opportunity map, and why single-digit races just doubled.

> **Summary for citation:** In DecaGEO's week of August 30, 2026 data (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US, 10 categories, 413 ranked brand slots), two of ten category #1s changed, and both were reversals of last week's changes: Dynamics 365 Sales retook CRM #1 from HubSpot Sales Hub, whose reign lasted one week, and Zendesk Suite resolved the Help Desk tie in its favor, leading Freshdesk by 2.7 points while Freshdesk kept the higher mention rate (67.1% vs 62.0%). ClickUp defended Project Management #1, the second consecutive-week hold of the GPT-5.6 era after Jira's in late July. Semrush widened its GEO lead from 13.4 to 17.7 points as Profound's mention rate fell back from 57.1% to 46.0%. Klaviyo cut Brevo's Email mention-rate gap from 5.1 points to 1.3 while the DECA gap narrowed to 7.8. Adobe Photoshop's \+20.3 was the week's biggest gain, reversing last week's −14.5. Zoho CRM's −25.6 was the biggest fall. The DECA Volatility Index was 9.9%, a fifth straight week below 10%.

> **Key takeaway:** Takeovers keep failing their first defense. Across the GPT-5.6 era, six #1 takeovers have now faced a next-week test and four lasted exactly one week — HubSpot's CRM reign this week joined Profound's, ClickUp's, and Jira's August one-week reigns, and Freshdesk's tie unwound the same way. The exceptions are both on Project Management, the board that flips most. What did keep advancing was frequency: Klaviyo now trails Brevo by 1.3 points of mention rate, the narrowest that signal has been for any challenger we track, and single-digit DECA races doubled from two to four.

**Last week's two changes at the top both rolled back.** In [CRM](https://decageo.ai/category/crm), [Dynamics 365 Sales](https://decageo.ai/products/dynamics-365-sales/category/crm) retook #1 one week after its six-week run ended, and the 5.0-point race inverted into a 10.6-point Dynamics lead. The mention-rate lead moved back with it (Dynamics 46.8% vs [HubSpot Sales Hub](https://decageo.ai/products/hubspot-sales-hub/category/crm)'s 38.0%, reversed from last week's 43.0% vs 48.1%). Below the flip, [Zoho CRM](https://decageo.ai/products/zoho-crm/category/crm) posted the week's biggest fall anywhere (−25.6, #3 to #4). In [Help Desk](https://decageo.ai/category/help-desk), the 100.0 tie resolved in [Zendesk Suite](https://decageo.ai/products/zendesk-suite/category/help-desk)'s favor: sole #1 by 2.7 points, now the smallest #1 gap on any board. The signals stayed split, though — [Freshdesk](https://decageo.ai/products/freshdesk/category/help-desk) at #2 still has the higher mention rate (67.1% vs 62.0%), so Zendesk leads on prominence while Freshdesk leads on frequency.

**ClickUp defended Project Management while the board reshuffled beneath it.** [ClickUp](https://decageo.ai/products/clickup/category/project-management) held #1 (100, 48.1% mention rate) — its first back-to-back defense, and only the second consecutive-week hold of the GPT-5.6 era after Jira's in the model's first two weeks. ## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT in the US region and records which brands are named and how prominently, across 10 software categories. This week's data was collected on August 27, 2026 on GPT-5.6 (model label `gpt-5.6-terra`), the sixth GPT-5.6 collection. Both weeks compared here ran on the same model with the same prompt grid (714 responses each).

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, based on how frequently and how prominently a brand is named. Scores are normalized weekly so each category's leader sits at 100. A score change therefore reads as a change in the gap to the leader, not an absolute shift.
- **Mention rate** is the share of responses that name the brand, reported alongside DECA where frequency and prominence diverge — as they do this week in Help Desk, where the #1 on DECA trails on mention rate.
- **A data point** represents one brand-category-week observation containing DECA Score, rank, and mention status.
- **Model labels** (e.g., GPT-5.6) reflect the visible model identifier at the time of each weekly collection, not independently verified model versions.
- **Held-vs-changed verdicts** compare this week against prior GPT-5.6 weeks. Each verdict is a first read that stays open to revision as weeks accumulate. Rank-move counts (±10) are computed among brands present in both weeks (322 this week).
- **Limitations:** Week-over-week counts partly reflect newly detected brands and may include re-aggregation noise, and boards with high turnover (GEO again this week, with 28 entries) are the most exposed to it. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend. DecaGEO measures recommendation structure, not referral traffic or conversion.
