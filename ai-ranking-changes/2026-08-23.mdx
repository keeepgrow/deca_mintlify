## What this article covers

Each week this report separates what held from what changed. This week the change concentrated at the very top of the boards: [HubSpot Sales Hub](https://decageo.ai/products/hubspot-sales-hub/category/crm) ended Dynamics 365 Sales' six-week [CRM](https://decageo.ai/category/crm) run, [ClickUp](https://decageo.ai/products/clickup/category/project-management) retook [Project Management](https://decageo.ai/category/project-management) for the board's fourth #1 change in five weeks, and [Freshdesk](https://decageo.ai/products/freshdesk/category/help-desk) pulled level with Zendesk Suite at the top of [Help Desk](https://decageo.ai/category/help-desk). All of it happened in the quietest week the GPT-5.6 index has recorded: 8.9% volatility. Below: the biggest moves, the held-vs-changed table, a category opportunity map, and what this week does to the "double-digit cushions hold" pattern.

This week two of those leads failed to protect the top: Dynamics 365 Sales led CRM by 17 points and lost #1, and Zendesk led Help Desk by 19.1 and was pulled level. In both cases the challenger also took the mention-rate lead — the frequency signal Klaviyo has not yet flipped in Email. Meanwhile Project Management's 7.6-point race resolved by changing hands, and a new single-digit race formed in CRM.

**Two #1s changed hands: CRM after six weeks, Project Management for the fourth time in five.** In [CRM](https://decageo.ai/category/crm), [HubSpot Sales Hub](https://decageo.ai/products/hubspot-sales-hub/category/crm) rose \+18.5 from #3 to #1, ending a six-week run — [Dynamics 365 Sales](https://decageo.ai/products/dynamics-365-sales/category/crm) had held CRM #1 since mid-July and led by 17 points as recently as last week. The mention-rate lead moved with the flip (HubSpot 48.1% vs Dynamics 43.0%, reversed from 40.5% vs 46.8%), and at 5.0 points the gap is now the narrowest #1 race on any board. In [Project Management](https://decageo.ai/category/project-management), [ClickUp](https://decageo.ai/products/clickup/category/project-management) retook #1 from [Jira](https://decageo.ai/products/jira/category/project-management), whose −25.9 (#1 to #5) was the week's biggest fall. That is the board's fourth #1 change in five weeks, and no Project Management leader has yet held the top for two consecutive weeks on GPT-5.6. [Smartsheet](https://decageo.ai/products/smartsheet/category/project-management) moved #5 to #2, tied with Asana at 78.7.

**Two challenges advanced — one to a tie at the top, one into single digits.** In [Help Desk](https://decageo.ai/category/help-desk), [Freshdesk](https://decageo.ai/products/freshdesk/category/help-desk)'s third run at [Zendesk Suite](https://decageo.ai/products/zendesk-suite/category/help-desk) went further than the first two: \+19.1 to 100.0, level with Zendesk at the top of the board, with the mention-rate lead back on Freshdesk's side (64.6% vs 55.7%). After two receded challenges (gaps of 3.3 and 19.1 at their turns), this one reached the top line. In [Email Marketing](https://decageo.ai/category/email-marketing), [Klaviyo](https://decageo.ai/products/klaviyo/category/email-marketing) cut [Brevo Marketing Platform](https://decageo.ai/products/brevo-marketing-platform/category/email-marketing)'s DECA lead from 20.7 to 8.9 points — the prominence gap moving back toward its 1.1-point low of three weeks ago — while the mention-rate gap stayed where it was (5.1 points). Brevo's #1, held every tracked week, is intact.

**The week's biggest gains clustered on the GEO board, which also churned the most.** [HubSpot AEO](https://decageo.ai/products/hubspot-aeo/category/geo-generative-engine-optimization) posted the week's biggest gain anywhere (\+19.7, to #5), and [Peec.ai](https://decageo.ai/products/peec.ai/category/geo-generative-engine-optimization) (\+16.9) moved up to #2, tied at 77.2 with [Scrunch AI](https://decageo.ai/products/scrunch-ai/category/geo-generative-engine-optimization) behind [Profound](https://decageo.ai/products/profound/category/geo-generative-engine-optimization), whose mention rate rose from 46.0% to 57.1% of responses at the top of the board. The [GEO](https://decageo.ai/category/geo-generative-engine-optimization) board also turned over more than any we have tracked: 30 brands out, 21 in, only 17 present in both weeks, and the count fell from 47 to 38. Turnover on that scale re-bases DECA scores across the board — it is why Scrunch AI shows one of the week's biggest score falls (−13.5) while holding #2.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT in the US region and records which brands are named and how prominently, across 10 software categories. This week's data was collected on August 20, 2026 on GPT-5.6 (model label `gpt-5.6-terra`), the fifth GPT-5.6 collection. Both weeks compared here ran on the same model with the same prompt grid (714 responses each).

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, based on how frequently and how prominently a brand is named. Scores are normalized weekly so each category's leader sits at 100. A score change therefore reads as a change in the gap to the leader, not an absolute shift — this matters most on GEO this week, where high turnover re-based the whole board.
- **Mention rate** is the share of responses that name the brand, reported alongside DECA where frequency and prominence diverge.
- **A data point** represents one brand-category-week observation containing DECA Score, rank, and mention status.
- **Model labels** (e.g., GPT-5.6) reflect the visible model identifier at the time of each weekly collection, not independently verified model versions.
- **Held-vs-changed verdicts** compare this week against prior GPT-5.6 weeks. Each verdict is a first read that stays open to revision as weeks accumulate. Rank-move counts (±10) are computed among brands present in both weeks (324 this week).
- **Limitations:** Week-over-week counts partly reflect newly detected brands and may include re-aggregation noise, and boards with high turnover (GEO this week) are the most exposed to it. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend. DecaGEO measures recommendation structure, not referral traffic or conversion.
