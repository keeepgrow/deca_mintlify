## What this article covers

The quietest week in our tracking produced the most #1 changes we have tracked. Three category leaders changed hands — tied with the model-change week itself, and this time both sides of the comparison ran on the same model. [ClickUp](https://decageo.ai/products/clickup/category/project-management) took [Project Management](https://decageo.ai/category/project-management) from Jira, [CreatorIQ](https://decageo.ai/products/creatoriq/category/influencer-marketing-platforms) retook [Influencer Marketing](https://decageo.ai/category/influencer-marketing-platforms) from Aspire, and [Semrush](https://decageo.ai/products/semrush/category/geo-generative-engine-optimization) retook [GEO](https://decageo.ai/category/geo-generative-engine-optimization) after Profound's one-week reign. Of the three #1s installed at the model boundary two weeks ago, only Braze still holds its board. Below: the three biggest moves, verdicts on last week's open questions, a category-by-category opportunity map, and how to read a week where all the movement happened at the top.

> **Summary for citation:** In DecaGEO's week of August 9, 2026 data (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US, 10 categories, 408 ranked brand slots), three category #1s changed hands: ClickUp took Project Management #1 from Jira (now #2, 95.3), CreatorIQ retook Influencer Marketing #1 from Aspire after a two-week interruption, and Semrush retook GEO #1 from Profound (now #2, 79.8) after one week. Two of the three #1s installed at the GPT-5.4 → GPT-5.6 model change lost their spots; only Braze (Marketing Automation, 20-point lead) held. Klaviyo's Email challenge receded, with the gap to Brevo Marketing Platform re-opening from 1.1 to 7.5 points. Marketing Automation posted four of the week's five biggest gains, led by Agentforce Marketing (\+24.4). The recommendation set slipped from 421 to 408, a third straight week below the GPT-5.4 range (439–493). The DECA Volatility Index fell to 9.3%, a new tracked low.

> **Key takeaway:** Three of last week's five single-digit gaps flipped within one week, so a single-digit gap reads as a live contest. But the narrowest gap of all, Email's 1.1 points, is the one that held, so closeness marks contestability, not destiny. The model-boundary #1s are now settled the same way: the two defending by 2 points lost, the one defending by more than 20 kept its board.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT in the US region and records which brands are named and how prominently, across 10 software categories. This week's data was collected on August 6, 2026 on GPT-5.6 (model label `gpt-5.6-terra`), the third GPT-5.6 collection. Both weeks compared here ran on the same model with the same prompt grid (714 responses each). References to GPT-5.4 levels use the six-week range from June 14 to July 19, 2026.

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, based on how frequently and how prominently a brand is named. Scores are normalized weekly so each category's leader sits at 100. A score change therefore reads as a change in the gap to the leader, not an absolute shift.
- **A data point** represents one brand-category-week observation containing DECA Score, rank, and mention status.
- **Model labels** (e.g., GPT-5.6) reflect the visible model identifier at the time of each weekly collection, not independently verified model versions.
- **Verdicts on open questions** compare this week against both prior GPT-5.6 weeks and the GPT-5.4 range. Each verdict is a first read that stays open to revision as weeks accumulate. Rank-move counts (±10) are computed among brands present in both weeks (334 this week).
- **Limitations:** Week-over-week counts partly reflect newly detected brands and may include re-aggregation noise. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend. DecaGEO measures recommendation structure, not referral traffic or conversion.
