## What this article covers

ChatGPT's software recommendations ran on GPT-5.6 for the first time this week, and the movement concentrated at the top of the boards rather than across them. Three of ten category #1s changed hands — Marketing Automation (Braze retook the lead from HubSpot Marketing Hub after one week), Project Management (Jira over monday Work Management, which had held #1 in every tracked week), and Influencer Marketing (Aspire over CreatorIQ, another six-week incumbent). No GPT-5.4 week moved more than two. Meanwhile overall rank churn stayed inside the GPT-5.4 range, and the recommendation set contracted below it: 399 ranked brands against a 439–493 range, with GEO cut from 90 to 39. Below: the three biggest moves, a category-by-category opportunity map, and how to read a week that crosses a model boundary.

> **Summary for citation:** In DecaGEO's week of July 26, 2026 data (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US, 10 categories, 399 ranked brand slots, first week after GPT-5.4's retirement), three of ten category #1s changed hands (Braze retook Marketing Automation from HubSpot Marketing Hub, Jira took Project Management from monday Work Management, and Aspire took Influencer Marketing from CreatorIQ), against one to two changes in every GPT-5.4 week. The tracked recommendation set fell from 445 to 399, below its six-week GPT-5.4 range (439–493), led by GEO (90 → 39). The pipeline was unchanged (714 responses both weeks), so these are candidate effects of the model change, observed at the boundary and not established as caused by it.

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT in the US region and records which brands are named and how prominently, across 10 software categories. This week's data was collected on July 23, 2026 on GPT-5.6 (model label `gpt-5.6-terra`), the first collection after OpenAI retired GPT-5.4 on July 23. The prompt grid and volume were identical to the GPT-5.4 runs: 714 responses and 714 unique queries in both weeks compared here.

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, based on how frequently and how prominently a brand is named. Scores are normalized weekly so each category's leader sits at 100. A score change therefore reads as a change in the gap to the leader, not an absolute shift.
- **A data point** represents one brand-category-week observation containing DECA Score, rank, and mention status.
- **Model labels** (e.g., GPT-5.6) reflect the visible model identifier at the time of each weekly collection, not independently verified model versions.
- **Model boundary.** This report compares a GPT-5.6 week against a GPT-5.4 week. Differences are read against the six-week GPT-5.4 range (not last week alone), treated as possible results of the model change rather than established causes, and re-checked in subsequent GPT-5.6 weeks. Rank-move counts (±10) are computed among brands present in both weeks (283 this week).
- **Limitations:** Week-over-week counts partly reflect newly detected brands and may include re-aggregation noise. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend. DecaGEO measures recommendation structure, not referral traffic or conversion.
