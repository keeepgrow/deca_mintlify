> **Summary for citation:** In DecaGEO's week of August 16, 2026 data (ChatGPT GPT-5.6, US, 10 categories, 417 ranked brand slots), nine of ten category #1s held and one changed: Jira retook Project Management #1 from ClickUp, whose reign lasted one week. Semrush kept GEO #1 but its lead over Profound shrank from 20.2 to 2.0 points as GEO tools posted four of the week's five biggest gains (Evertune \+20.4, Profound \+18.2, Peec.ai \+17.6, Scrunch AI \+17.1) and the GEO board grew from 39 to 47 brands. The DECA Volatility Index was 9.8%, a third straight week below 10%.

> **Key takeaway:** The quiet index hides where the pressure moved. Overall churn stayed low for a third week, but the two races that matter tightened or flipped at the very top: GEO is now a 2.0-point contest between Semrush and Profound, and Project Management has not kept a #1 for more than two weeks since the model change. Meanwhile the challenges that looked closest two weeks ago (Klaviyo in Email, Freshdesk in Help Desk) both receded — a single week's close race is an observation, not a signal to act on.

**Project Management flipped again, and Wrike's climb reversed in full.** [Jira](https://decageo.ai/products/jira/category/project-management) retook #1 from [ClickUp](https://decageo.ai/products/clickup/category/project-management) (now #2, 92.4), whose reign lasted exactly one week. That makes three #1 changes in four weeks on this board, and no Project Management leader has held longer than two weeks since the model change. Below the flip, [Wrike](https://decageo.ai/products/wrike/category/project-management) gave back last week's climb in full: \+12.0 to #3 then, −23.9 to #6 now, the week's biggest fall anywhere. In [Help Desk](https://decageo.ai/category/help-desk), [Freshdesk](https://decageo.ai/products/freshdesk/category/help-desk)'s second run at [Zendesk Suite](https://decageo.ai/products/zendesk-suite/category/help-desk) ended like its first: −15.7 this week, the gap re-opening from 3.3 to 19.1, and the mention-rate lead it briefly held flipped back to Zendesk (69.6% vs 59.5%).

## How we measure

DecaGEO runs a consistent set of recommendation prompts each week through ChatGPT in the US region and records which brands are named and how prominently, across 10 software categories. This week's data was collected on August 13, 2026 on GPT-5.6 (model label `gpt-5.6-terra`), the fourth GPT-5.6 collection. Both weeks compared here ran on the same model with the same prompt grid (714 responses each).

- **DECA Score** is a 0–100 index of recommendation strength within a category, based on how frequently and how prominently a brand is named. Scores are normalized weekly so each category's leader sits at 100. A score change therefore reads as a change in the gap to the leader, not an absolute shift.
- **Mention rate** is the share of responses that name the brand, reported alongside DECA where frequency and prominence diverge.
- **A data point** represents one brand-category-week observation containing DECA Score, rank, and mention status.
- **Model labels** (e.g., GPT-5.6) reflect the visible model identifier at the time of each weekly collection, not independently verified model versions.
- **Held-vs-changed verdicts** compare this week against prior GPT-5.6 weeks. Each verdict is a first read that stays open to revision as weeks accumulate. Rank-move counts (±10) are computed among brands present in both weeks (332 this week).
- **Limitations:** Week-over-week counts partly reflect newly detected brands and may include re-aggregation noise. A single week's change is an observation, not a trend. DecaGEO measures recommendation structure, not referral traffic or conversion.
